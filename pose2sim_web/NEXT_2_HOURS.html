Sentence to write: "Pilot validation on one participant, 9 static postures from one trial, two observers' repeated manual Kinovea measurement of taped joints (n = 27 angle pairs). Pose2Sim angles differed by X deg (bias) with 95 % limits of agreement from A to B deg; MAE X deg."

Limitations to write honestly

  • One participant, one trial, one camera setup: shows feasibility, not general accuracy. Static postures only.
  • Kinovea is a manual 2D measurement, not a motion-capture gold standard; it is affected by tape placement and camera perspective.
  • Pipeline log: mean reprojection error 3.8 px (about 8.7 mm); lens distortion was not taken into account in triangulation; no limb-swap handling; the 2 cameras were 2.90 m apart at 89 deg.
  • Pose keypoints (RTMPose HALPE-26) are not at the tape position, so a small constant bias is expected.
  • Calibration: intrinsic RMS about 0.35 px; extrinsic from a board lying on the floor.
