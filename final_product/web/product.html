Recording checklist

  • Two cameras about 90° apart: one in front, one at the side, both at hip height.
  • Whole body in the picture, feet included, for every rep.
  • Same resolution and frame rate as the intrinsic videos (e.g. 1920×1080, 29.97 fps).
  • Board held still for 2–3 s where both cameras see it, then one loud clap.
  • About 10 reps at your normal pace; stand up fully between reps.
